English language entry requirements

Courses IELTS (Academic) Pearson Test of English (PTE) - Academic TOEFL iBT Kaplan Test of English (KTE) Duolingo English Test (DET) Michigan English Test (MET)
Foundation Program 5.5 overall score (no band less than 5.0) Overall score of 45 (with a minimum 48 in Reading, 43 in Listening, 42 in Speaking and 51 in Writing) Overall score of 46 (with a minimum of 8 in Reading, 7 in Listening, 16 in Speaking and 18 in Writing) Overall Score of 410 (with a minimum 410 in Reading, 410 in Listening, 410 in Speaking and 410 in Writing) Overall score of 100 (with a minimum scores of 85 in Reading, Speaking, and Listening, and 80 in Writing) Overall score of 49 (with minimum scores of 47 in Reading, 45 in Writing, 38 in Speaking, and 49 in Listening)
Diploma 5.5 overall score (no band less than 5.0) Overall score of 45 (with a minimum 48 in Reading, 43 in Listening, 42 in Speaking and 51 in Writing) Overall score of 46 (with a minimum of 8 in Reading, 7 in Listening, 16 in Speaking and 18 in Writing) Overall of Score 410 (with a minimum 410 in Reading, 410 in Listening, 410 in Speaking and 410 in Writing) Overall score of 100 (with a minimum scores of 85 in Reading, Speaking, and Listening, and 80 in Writing) Overall score of 49 (with minimum scores of 47 in Reading, 45 in Writing, 38 in Speaking, and 49 in Listening)
All undergraduate and postgraduate courses with standard requirements 6.0 overall score (no band less than 6.0) Overall score of 50 (with a minimum 53 in Reading, 50 in Listening, 50 in Speaking and 54 in Writing) Overall score of 60 (with a minimum of 13 in Reading, 12 in Listening, 18 in Speaking and 21 in Writing) Overall score of 444 (with a minimum 444 in Reading, 444 in Listening, 444 in Speaking and 444 in Writing) Overall score of 115 (with minimum scores of 110 in Reading, Writing, and Speaking, and 105 in Listening) Overall score of 53 (with minimum scores of 55 in Reading, 57 in Writing, 48 in Speaking, and 56 in Listening)
Courses with higher entry requirements: Master of Education (MEd) and Graduate Certificates in Education 6.5 overall score (no band less than 6.0) Overall score of 58 (with a minimum 53 in Reading, 50 in Listening, 50 in Speaking and 58 in Writing) Overall score of 79 (with a minimum of 13 in Reading, 12 in Listening, 18 in Speaking and 21 in Writing) Overall Score of 478 (with a minimum score of 444 in Reading, 444 in Listening, 444 in Speaking, and 444 in Writing) Overall score of 120 (with minimum scores of 110 in Reading, Writing, and Speaking, and 105 in Listening) Overall score of 58 (with minimum scores of 55 in Reading, 57 in Writing, 48 in Speaking, and 56 in Listening)
