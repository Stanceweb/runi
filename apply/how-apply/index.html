How to apply to Russian University

We know that the application process can be daunting, but we're here to guide you through it with our special university application guide. Here are the main application steps you should follow:

Russian University application steps

STEP 1

Choose your course and check the academic and English language proficiency entry criteria for our foundation, undergraduate, and graduate programs.

STEP 2

Submit the required documents:

  • An official copy of your academic transcript (both original and English translated versions if applicable)
  • A certified copy of your exam results in English (if available and applicable)
  • Copy of Passport
  • Visa replica (for students in the UAE only)

Note: You might need to submit your CV, a statement of intent, and proof of work experience for some postgraduate programs.

STEP 3

Submit your application to Russian University through either of the below options:

  • Directly submit an application directly using our online form.
  • Apply through a local agent for overseas education.
STEP 4

Receive your Conditional or Unconditional Letter of Offer

If your application is accepted, you will receive a Letter of Offer outlining the course(s) you have been accepted for, the fees due, details about any scholarships, requirements to be fulfilled (if any), and the start date and duration of the course.

STEP 5

Accept your offer

You must make sure you’ve satisfied all the academic and English requirements outlined on the offer, pay the tuition fees, sign the acceptance form, and email it back to admissions@russianuniuae.com in order to accept the offer and reserve your spot at Russian University. Russian University will send you emails with information regarding enrolment and orientation as soon as you accept your spot.

Our admissions team is available to help you every step of the way. Please call us at +971 4 574 9800 or send us an email at admissions@russianuniuae.com if you have any additional questions.
