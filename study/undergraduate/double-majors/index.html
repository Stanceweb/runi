The best of both worlds

Russian University’s flexible learning options are tailored to allow students to design their course of study in accordance with your own personal requirements.

Our double major option allows you to combine two different courses into one well-rounded education, without extending your time at university, for the price of one degree.*

Note: Diploma students choosing to do a double major across Business and Communication may be required to take additional units once they progress to the undergraduate course. Please contact us for more information.

Why should you consider a Double Major?

  • Stand out from the crowd as a double major could lead to more job opportunities and higher earnings. A study published by Cambridge University Press found that students who double major in business and a STEM field typically earn more than those who have just one major.
  • Create your own educational journey. You’ll get a more well-rounded education and a unique skill set you can use in your career.
  • You will learn additional skills, perspectives, and concepts which can then be applied and favourable in your professional and personal life.
  • We are the only university in the UAE offering double major combinations across all our undergraduate programs.
